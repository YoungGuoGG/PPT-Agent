# Design Contract

Produce a DesignPackage containing:

- goal, audience, language, canvas, page count, and assumptions;
- narrative arc and page-by-page briefs;
- page role, information priority, rhythm, density, and layout intent;
- color tokens, typography tokens, spacing, safe areas, and icon policy;
- image/formula/chart requirements and acquisition status;
- source claim references for every content page;
- style-case references and permitted borrowing scope.

Do not pause for confirmation. Pick conservative defaults from the request and source, record them as assumptions, and continue.

The machine-readable lock is authoritative during SVG generation. A page may vary its layout and rhythm but must not invent new global tokens.
